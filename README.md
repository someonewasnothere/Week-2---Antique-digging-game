CONTROLS:
    -click the browns squares which is the digging zone
    -you can click one square 4 times before there is no more space to be dug there
    -dig to find fossils (you can see the different types in the fossil shop)
    -the way to pick up a fossil is a bit funky, so you will have to completely dig the spot where the fossil is(this means that you have to completely hollow out the space of the fossil)
    -fossils are of different sizes and cannot overlap
    -you can sell what fossils you collect for coins
    -be CAREFUL about your digs because you only have 40 per run

    -when you run out of digs, you can click the new dig site button to restart the digging plot, regenerating the fossils, and how many times you can dig

OBJECTIVES:
    -collect all of the fossils in the index
    -buy the Ultimate TerraCoin to prove your mastery



HOW TO RUN:
    -Runs on a browser (its ran with HTML)
    -Works on laptop for sure, should work on mobile too i think
